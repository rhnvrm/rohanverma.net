A collection of cognitive biases, psychological effects, laws, and thinking tools. Some are well-known, some are obscure. All are worth having names for.

---

### Biases & Effects

[Baader-Meinhof Effect](@/pages/mental-models/baader-meinhof-effect.md) — the frequency illusion. You learn a new word, suddenly it's everywhere.

[Barnum Effect](@/pages/mental-models/barnum-effect.md) — why horoscopes feel accurate. Vague statements accepted as personal.

[Dunning-Kruger Effect](@/pages/mental-models/dunning-kruger-effect.md) — low skill, high confidence. High skill, low confidence.

[Nocebo](@/pages/mental-models/nocebo.md) — the opposite of placebo. Expecting harm causes harm.

### Laws & Principles

[Hyrum's Law](@/pages/mental-models/hyrums-law.md) — with enough users, every observable behavior becomes a contract.

[Forgetting Is the Norm](@/pages/mental-models/forgetting-is-the-norm.md) — we lose most details. The question is why we remember anything at all.
